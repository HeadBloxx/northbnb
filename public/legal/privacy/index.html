Who we are

NorthB&B is operated by Trip North LLC ("NorthB&B", "we", "us", "our"), based at Gradski Trg bb, Rožaje, Montenegro. We provide an online platform that connects travellers with private accommodation across Montenegro and helps property owners list and manage their stays. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information collected through https://northbnb.com and related services (the "Service").

This policy is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as well as applicable Montenegrin data-protection law. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the section on your rights applies to you in full.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Booking & enquiry details: such as your name, email, phone number, nationality, travel dates and the property or destination you are interested in.
  • Host applications: if you apply to list a property, the details you submit about the property, its location and capacity, your contact details and your preferred payout method.
  • Messages: the content of any contact form, email or message you send us.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you choose to subscribe.

Payment card details are handled by our payment providers and are not collected or stored by us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical & usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and the dates/times of your visits, collected to keep the Service secure and working.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: see our Cookie Notice for details.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the stays, bookings and host services you ask for.
  • To set up and manage host listings and arrange payouts to hosts.
  • To send service messages, and, where you have opted in, updates and offers.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the Service, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Our legal bases (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (to provide services you request); your consent (for marketing emails and non-essential cookies, which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests (to run, secure and improve the Service, balanced against your rights); and compliance with legal obligations.

When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • With hosts and guests: to arrange and fulfil a booking between you and the relevant party.
  • With service providers: hosting (Cloudflare), our booking platform provider, email delivery, and payment providers (for example PayPal and Payoneer) who process payments and host payouts under their own privacy terms.
  • For legal reasons: where required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA/UK. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. For example, enquiry and lead records are retained for a limited period to follow up and meet our obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymised. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see your rights below).

How we protect information

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls, input validation and rate limiting. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any incident.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to: access the personal information we hold about you; have it corrected or deleted; restrict or object to certain processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email us at info@northbnb.com.

If you are in the EEA or UK and believe we have not handled your information properly, you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Montenegro, this is the Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information (AZLP).

Cookies

We use only essential cookies and similar technologies needed to run the site. We do not currently use advertising or third-party analytics cookies. Full details, and how to control cookies, are in our Cookie Notice.

Children

The Service is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you. Please check back periodically.

How to contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: email info@northbnb.com, call +382 69 289 866, or write to Trip North LLC, Gradski Trg bb, Rožaje, Montenegro.
