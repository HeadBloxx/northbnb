Cookie Notice

How NorthB&B uses cookies and similar technologies on https://northbnb.com.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help a site work, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used. Some cookies are "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) and some are "persistent" (they stay until they expire or you delete them).

How we use cookies

NorthB&B keeps cookie use to a minimum. We currently use only strictly necessary storage, for example, remembering that you have dismissed our cookie banner, and the technical essentials needed to serve pages securely. We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party analytics that track you across sites.

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent first.

How to control cookies

Because we only use essential storage, the site will not work correctly if it is blocked entirely. You can still manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or remove cookies and clear site data. Links to common browsers' instructions are available in their own help pages.

Third-party services

When you proceed to pay or are handed off to our booking system, those providers (for example our booking platform, PayPal or Payoneer) may set their own cookies under their own policies. Please refer to their notices for details.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Notice as our practices change. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
