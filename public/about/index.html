A booking platform with its feet in Montenegro

We're a small team that connects travellers with handpicked private places to stay, and helps local owners reach them directly.

Local knowledge, not a global catalogue

NorthB&B is operated by Trip North LLC, based in Rožaje. We bring together apartments, villas, family-run hotels, hostels and campgrounds from across the country, from the Adriatic coast to the northern mountains, and make them easy to book directly, at the real rate.

Because we work hand-in-hand with hosts rather than listing millions of places sight-unseen, we can keep prices honest, listings verified, and support genuinely personal.

Co-funded by the European Union as part of a wider effort to grow sustainable tourism in the region.

Five things we don't compromise on

Best prices

Direct relationships with hosts mean the rate you see is the real one. No marketplace markup layered on top.

Payment security

Pay by major card or PayPal with confidence, or settle on arrival. Your details are handled carefully.

Verified properties

We know the places we list. Listings are checked, not auto-imported from a faceless feed.

A personal approach

Real people you can talk to before you book and throughout your trip, any hour of the day.

Local connection

We live and work here. That local knowledge is what turns a booking into a better trip.

Fair to hosts

Owners keep more and stay in control, with help to get set up and paid, worldwide.

Come stay, or come host

Whichever brings you here, we'd love to help.
