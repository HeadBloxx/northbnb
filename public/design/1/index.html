Questions, answered

Where in Montenegro can I book a stay?

Across the country: coastal towns such as Herceg Novi and Budva, the capital Podgorica, and mountain spots near Žabljak. Search by town and dates to see what's available.

How do I pay for a booking?

By major credit card or PayPal online, or on arrival at the property. You choose the method that suits your trip.

Is booking with NorthB&B cheaper than the big travel sites?

We work directly with hosts, so there's no added marketplace markup. The rate you see is the real, best rate.

Can I get help if something comes up during my trip?

Yes. Our support team is available 24/7 to help you choose and book, and to sort out anything that comes up while you travel.

How do I list my own property?

Use the guided host signup. It takes a few minutes. We handle the setup and a real person helps you get your listing live.
